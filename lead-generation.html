Understand Where Leads Are Lost

Small points of friction create unnecessary barriers.

Potential customers may leave when:

I help identify relevant issues and prioritize practical improvements based on the available evidence.

  • Your offer isn't clear.
  • Your website doesn't establish enough trust.
  • Your landing page doesn't match the advertisement.
  • Your contact form asks for too much information.
  • Your calls to action are difficult to find.
  • Your website experience is frustrating on mobile.
  • You cannot reliably measure important conversions.
